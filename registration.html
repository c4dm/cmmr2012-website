Full week and One day registrations

For each paper, poster and demonstration paper, at least one author must register for the whole conference by the early registration deadline (see Important dates section) for the paper to be included in the conference proceedings.

  • Full week registrations (19 to 22 June 2012) include attendance to all technical sessions (paper, poster, demo, panel, tutorial/workshop, the special workshop on expressive music performance), social program, three CMMR 2012 concert tickets held on evenings at Wilton's Music Hall, lunch meals, coffee breaks, and the gala dinner.
  • 1 day registration includes attendance to the technical sessions of the selected day, the conference proceedings in electronic format, goodies, 1 CMMR 2012 concert ticket held on evening at Wilton's Music Hall, a lunch meal, and coffee breaks.

Both registration types include the conference proceedings in electronic format and goodies.

Student discount

Students receive a discount rate and must send a proof of their student status by email to Tom Heathcote, at: tom.heathcote@eecs.qmul.ac.uk 

Please do so before or soon after registration, and indicate "CMMR 2012 student proof" in the subject of the email.

During online registration, you will be presented with an option to book a room on the campus from Monday 18th June 2012 to Saturday 23rd June 2012, subject to availability. New Resonances Music Festival

New Resonances: Music, Machines and Emotions is a series of three concerts and media installations at Wilton's Music Hall that are part of the CMMR 2012 conference. The concerts present some of the first performances of new works and installations made possible by scientific advances.

  • Full week registration includes tickets for the three New Resonances evening concerts.
  • 1 day registration includes one concert ticket for one of the New Resonances evening concerts at your choice.
  • You will be given the opportunity to book additional concert tickets when registering online.

Please refer to the conference programme page for dates and times of the concerts. The concert's venue (Wilton's Music Hall) will open its doors and Mahogahany Bar before the start of the concerts (the doors opening times will be announced on Wilton's website).

A unique blues/rock live performance involving interactive technologies will be given at Wilton's from 9pm to 10pm on Friday 22nd June 2012, closing the New Resonances Music Festival, and the CMMR 2012 conference.
