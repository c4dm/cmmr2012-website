The 9th International Symposium on Computer Music Modeling and Retrieval offers support or sponsorship opportunities for interested organizations. Your support will be clearly visible to the key personnel in the field of music informatics and related fields, with acknowledgements on the conference website, at the conference venue, on the  conference goodies, and on scientific proceedings.

We propose the following support/sponsorship packages offering various types of benefits:

Bronze sponsorship: 500 GBP (incl. VAT)

  • Logo and links on conference website
  • Logo of small size on banners, program, goodies
  • Logo of small size in the conference proceedings
  • Acknowledgements in the post-proceedings
  • Advertisements in conference bags
  • Slot for a talk to present the company
 
Silver sponsorship: 1000 GBP (incl. VAT)
 
  • Logo and links on conference website
  • Logo of small size on banners, program, goodies
  • Logo of small size in the conference proceedings
  • Acknowledgements in the post-proceedings
  • Advertisements in conference bags
  • Exhibition space during demonstration sessions
  • Slot for a talk to present the company
  • 1 complimentary two-days conference registration (including gala dinner and concerts)
  • 1 complimentary concert ticket
 
Gold sponsorship: 2000 GBP (incl. VAT)
 
  • Logo and links on conference website
  • Logo of medium size on banners, program, goodies
  • Logo of medium size in the conference proceedings
  • Acknowledgements in the post-proceedings
  • Advertisements in conference bags
  • Exhibition space during demonstration sessions
  • Slot for a talk to present the company
  • 1 complimentary full-conference registration (including gala dinner and concerts)
  • 1 complimentary concert ticket
 
Platinum sponsorship: 3000 GBP (incl. VAT)
 
  • Logo and links on the conference website
  • Logo of large size on banners, program, goodies
  • Logo of large size in the conference proceedings
  • Acknowledgements in the post-proceedings
  • Advertisements in conference bags

  • 2 complimentary full-conference registrations (including gala dinner and concerts)
  • 2 complimentary concert tickets
 
Other forms of support/sponsorship

We welcome other forms of sponsorship to help the organisation of the conference and support delegates. This may include, but is not limited to, support for:

  • awards (e.g. gift, musical equipment) for best contributions (e.g. best paper, best poster, best oral presentation, best music piece)
  • student travel grants
  • conference T-shirts
  • catering or coffee break
  • social event (e.g. concert)

Such sponsorship will be acknowledged and made visible to the community through:

  • Logo and links on conference website (small, medium, or large size)
  • Logo on banners, program, goodies (small, medium, or large size)
  • Logo in the conference proceedings (small, medium, or large size)
  • Acknowledgements in the post-proceedings
  • Advertisements in conference bags
  • Talk to present the company
 
We also offer exhibition space.

500 GBP (+ VAT) for 4 days
